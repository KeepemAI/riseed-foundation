OUR STORY

Education is a path forward. For everyone.

RiseEd began with a simple belief: students facing disruption deserve academic support that is accessible, encouraging, and shaped by their communities.

OUR PURPOSE

Closing gaps caused by circumstance.

When natural disasters affect schools and families, learning can become harder to access. RiseEd aims to help address that challenge through a free virtual tutoring model designed in partnership with schools and local organizations.

Our approach begins with listening. We want to learn where additional academic support is useful, what families can access, and how to support students responsibly.

OUR PRINCIPLES

What guides our work

Equity

Academic support should not depend on a family's ability to pay.

Partnership

Schools and communities understand their students best; our program should reflect their needs.

Responsibility

Student safety, privacy, and volunteer preparation must come before program launch.

WHERE WE ARE TODAY

Building the foundation.

RiseEd is currently developing its first pilot, exploring partnerships, and planning volunteer recruitment. We will share verified program outcomes once tutoring begins.
