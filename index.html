THE PROBLEM

When disaster strikes, students are often the ones left behind.

Natural disasters can force schools to close, displace families, and disrupt the stability that children depend on to learn. While communities work to rebuild, many students face academic challenges through no fault of their own.

Why we started RiseEd →

THE HEART BEHIND RISEED

Every child deserves a fair chance to learn.

I believe every child deserves a good education. It isn't fair that a student can lose that opportunity because a wildfire, tornado, or flood changes their life overnight.

That's why I started RiseEd: to help students keep moving forward, even when everything around them feels uncertain.
