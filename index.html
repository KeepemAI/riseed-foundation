WHY RISEED EXISTS

Disruption shouldn't determine opportunity.

Natural disasters can force families to relocate, interrupt school routines, and create gaps in academic support. As communities recover, students deserve the chance to keep building confidence and skills.

RiseEd Foundation is a developing education initiative focused on connecting affected students with accessible, no-cost virtual tutoring—working alongside schools and communities, not replacing them.
