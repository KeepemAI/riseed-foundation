01 / WHY WE EXIST

Disasters change lives. They shouldn't define futures.

Wildfires, tornadoes, floods, and other emergencies can uproot families and interrupt school routines. As communities recover, students may need extra academic support.

RiseEd Foundation is an emerging initiative with a simple goal: connect students experiencing disaster-related educational disruption with accessible, high-quality virtual tutoring—at no cost to families.

Our vision: a world where every student has the support to keep learning, even through life's hardest interruptions.

02 / WHAT WE'RE BUILDING

Support that meets students where they are.

Our proposed pilot is designed to complement—not replace—the work of teachers and schools.

01

Free virtual tutoring

Planned one-on-one or small-group academic support, coordinated with participating schools and families.

02

Core academic subjects

Our initial focus is math, reading, writing, and other subjects identified by partner schools.

03

Thoughtful volunteer support

We plan to recruit and screen tutors, provide guidance, and establish appropriate child-safety and privacy practices before serving students.

Launch status: RiseEd is in the planning and partner-discovery stage. Program details will be finalized with pilot schools before tutoring begins.
