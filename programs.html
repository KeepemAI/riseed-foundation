OUR PROGRAM

Support that meets students where they are.

We’re designing a flexible virtual tutoring pilot for students experiencing disaster-related educational disruption. The details will be developed with participating schools.

PROPOSED PILOT

How tutoring could work

01

Identify student needs

School or community partners share where extra support may be helpful, without sending sensitive student data through public forms.

02

Plan accessible sessions

Agree on subject focus, format, scheduling, technology access, and family communication.

03

Prepare volunteers

Complete appropriate screening, training, safeguarding, and supervision arrangements before tutoring starts.

ACADEMIC FOCUS

Start with the essentials.

We expect our initial tutoring areas to include math, reading, and writing. Grade levels, group sizes, session length, and availability have not yet been finalized.

RiseEd’s goal is to complement classroom instruction—not replace teachers, school counseling, or specialized services.

Program status: This is a proposed pilot, not an active enrollment offering. We are not currently collecting student registrations.
