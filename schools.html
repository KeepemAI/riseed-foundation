Built around the students you know best.

We’re seeking conversations with schools, districts, and community organizations supporting students whose education has been interrupted by natural disasters.

A COLLABORATIVE PILOT

Simple first steps. Thoughtful planning.

Our first goal is to understand your community—not ask you to adopt a prepackaged solution.

Discuss a partnership ↗

Start a conversation

Share your community's needs and whether free virtual tutoring might be useful.

02

Co-design a pilot

Determine subject priorities, eligibility, scheduling, accessibility, and responsibilities.

03

Put safeguards in place

Establish tutor screening, adult oversight, consent, privacy, and communication procedures.

04

Evaluate and improve

If launched, review participation and feedback with partners before considering expansion.

WHO CAN REACH OUT?

Schools, libraries, and community organizations.

Whether your community is recovering from a wildfire, flood, tornado, or another emergency, we welcome an introductory conversation. A partnership is not guaranteed and program capacity is still being developed.
