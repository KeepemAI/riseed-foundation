VOLUNTEER WITH RISEED

Your time could help someone move forward.

We’re exploring a volunteer team of tutors, educators, and advisors who believe every student deserves academic opportunity.

WAYS TO CONTRIBUTE

Help us build something meaningful.

Prospective tutors

People with strong academic subject knowledge and an interest in supporting student learning.

Educators and advisors

Experienced adults who can help shape effective instruction, safeguarding, and program operations.

Community connectors

People who can introduce RiseEd to school leaders and organizations familiar with local needs.

IMPORTANT TO KNOW

Safety comes first.

Expressing interest does not mean you are approved to tutor. Before working with students, prospective volunteers will need to complete appropriate screening, training, and program requirements. Adult supervision and student privacy protections will be part of our planning.

For now, please email a brief introduction and the kind of support you might offer. Do not include student information.
